Critical 9.2 · Loss Indemnity and liability
Indemnity is uncapped and unqualified.
Clause 9.2 · as executed
“The Company and the Promoters shall jointly and severally indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Investor, its affiliates and their respective directors, officers and employees from and against any and all Loss suffered or incurred, directly or indirectly, in connection with any breach of any representation, warranty or covenant contained in this Agreement, without any monetary or temporal limitation, and any Loss arising under Clause 14.7.”
SHA, page 41. Clause 14.7 does not exist — see Proof.
Why it matters on this mandate
We act for the Investor, taking a minority stake at signing. An unlimited, joint and several indemnity with no basket, de minimis or cap is not the position the Investor accepted in its two prior Indian subscriptions, and the Company-side recourse erodes the Investor’s own holding. The absence of a temporal limit also keeps the Investor’s fund exposed past its expected exit.
Ask
Cap aggregate liability at the Subscription Amount, carve out fraud and wilful misconduct, and limit survival to 24 months (7 years for tax, title and authority).
Proposed language · optional
“Provided that the aggregate liability of the Company and the Promoters under this Clause 9 shall not exceed the Subscription Amount, and no claim shall be made after the expiry of twenty-four (24) months from the Closing Date, save in the case of fraud or wilful misconduct, or a claim in respect of tax, title or authority, where the period shall be seven (7) years.”
Drafting suggestion. Not adopted until you accept it.
