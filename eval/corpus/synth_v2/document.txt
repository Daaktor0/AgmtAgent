SHAREHOLDERS AGREEMENT
Helios Ventures Private Limited (the "Company") is the issuer.
1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
1.1 In this Agreement, headings are for convenience only.
2. ESCROW AND PAYMENT
2.1 The Investor shall pay the Subscription Amount into Escrow on Closing.
3. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT
3.1 The obligations of the Investor are conditional upon the Company delivering a closing certificate.
3.2 The Company shall use reasonable efforts to satisfy those conditions.
4. SHAREHOLDING
4.1 The shareholding of the parties is as follows:
Investor holds 40%.
Promoters hold 35%.
ESOP pool holds 15%.
5. AMOUNT DEFINITIONS
5.1 "Alpha" means the Beta Amount payable at Closing.
5.2 "Beta Amount" means the Alpha as adjusted for Leakage.
6. CONDUCT OF BUSINESS
6.1 Helios Ventures Ltd shall maintain its corporate existence.
7. COMPLETION MECHANICS
7.1 The completion mechanics are set out in Schedule 1.
8. NOTICES
8.1 The Company shall notify the Investor of any Leakage promptly.
9.
9.1 The Company shall keep books of account.
9.2 The Investor may inspect those books on reasonable notice.
10. LATE DEFINITIONS
10.1 "Escrow" means the account designated by the parties for the Subscription Amount.
SCHEDULE 1
Completion Mechanics
The Company shall deliver share certificates, the statutory registers, and board resolutions authorising allotment, and the Investor shall pay the Subscription Amount in immediately available funds to the account nominated in writing.
SCHEDULE 4
Unused Notes
Nil.
